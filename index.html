About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Public Finance at Zhongnan University of Economics and Law (ZUEL). My research focuses on fiscal policy for science and technology (S&T) and tax governance, with particular attention to how fiscal instruments shape innovation incentives and the modernization of tax administration.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I earned a B.A. in Economics and an M.A. in Public Finance from Zhongnan University of Economics and Law.

Research Interests

  • Primary: Science and Technology Fiscal Policy (科技财税)
  • Secondary: Tax Governance (税收治理)
